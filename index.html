The Challenge

AI is changing how scientific tools are built and used in science. This weekend is about testing how those systems can work reliably in real scientific workflows.

Mixed teams will use Codex with the BioNeMo Agent Toolkit to turn a credible life-sciences problem into a working repository, workflow, benchmark or prototype.

Build Inside the Labs

The weekend takes place inside WilbeLABS’ working London laboratories, keeping the scientific context close to the technical work.

Tracks

Teams will work across one of four tracks, with mentors, TAs and track leads from OpenAI and NVIDIA supporting them throughout the weekend.

01

OSS Build

Modernise or extend useful bioinformatics software by repackaging legacy tools, removing brittle dependencies or improving testing and documentation.

02

Orchestration

Combine specialist models and deterministic tools into repeatable workflows with clear inputs, outputs and human decisions.

03

Benchmarking

Make performance and failure modes visible on focused scientific tasks through representative tests, baselines and robustness checks.

04

Open Track

Bring a real wet-lab, bioinformatics or data-analysis problem and build the smallest useful prototype around it.

Who Should Apply

Teams will be formed across computational and biological expertise to build and learn together.

Builders

Software and ML engineers, computational scientists, bioinformaticians, technical scientists and founders working across AI and biology who want to build and test scientific workflows.

Scientists

Researchers, wet-lab scientists and life-science practitioners who bring biological questions, experimental knowledge and real-world scientific constraints.

Applications

Attendance is free. Space is limited, and attendees must be available for all three days.
